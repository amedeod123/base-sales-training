Cold chain & claims

When a load gets questioned, documentation wins. Ours is built in.

Produce disputes get decided on paper — PACA Good Arrival, pulp temps, recorder data. Most brokers learn that during their first claim. We built our whole process around it.

Temps in writing, every load

Operating temperature and sanitary requirements are specified in writing on every rate confirmation, in line with the FSMA Sanitary Transportation rule — and we keep the records.

Timestamped temp photos

Ingate temperature photos and documented pulp temps on refrigerated loads. If a question comes up at the dock, the evidence already exists.

PACA-fluent claims handling

We know Good Arrival guidelines and we speak the language of your claims process — so when something does go wrong, it gets resolved instead of litigated.

Vetted carriers, continuously

Every carrier is screened for authority, insurance, and safety before touching a load — and monitored continuously after, not just at onboarding.
