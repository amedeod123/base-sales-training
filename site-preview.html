Cold chain & claims

When a load gets questioned, documentation wins. Ours is built in.

Produce disputes get decided on paper — PACA Good Arrival, pulp temps, recorder data. Most brokers learn that during their first claim. We built our whole process around it.

Temps in writing, every load

Operating temperature and sanitary requirements are specified in writing on every rate confirmation, in line with the FSMA Sanitary Transportation rule — and we keep the records.

Timestamped temp photos

Ingate temperature photos and documented pulp temps on refrigerated loads. If a question comes up at the dock, the evidence already exists.

PACA-fluent claims handling

We know Good Arrival guidelines and we speak the language of your claims process — so when something does go wrong, it gets resolved instead of litigated.

Vetted carriers, continuously

Every carrier is screened for authority, insurance, and safety before touching a load — and monitored continuously after, not just at onboarding.

Straight answers

The questions a careful produce buyer actually asks.

What if a load arrives warm?

Every refrigerated load ships with the operating temperature in writing on the rate confirmation, a timestamped in-gate temperature photo, and a live GPS link. If a temperature question comes up at the dock, the evidence already exists — and you call the owner directly, not a claims department.

How do you handle produce claims?

We work to PACA Good Delivery guidelines and keep temperature records and pulp temps on every reefer load, so a claim gets resolved on documentation instead of litigated. We speak your claims language because produce is all we move.

What happens if the truck no-shows?

Most of our freight runs on weekly repeat lanes with carriers we've vetted and run before, and dispatch answers nights and weekends. If a truck falls through, we cover it off the Wilmington lanes we're running every single day.

How do I know BASE is legitimate?

BASE Carriers Transport LLC is a licensed property broker — MC-1299577, USDOT 3707179, BMC-84 bonded with the FMCSA. Look us up in SAFER. Insurance certificates and credit references are available the same day — we'd rather you check.

Do I have to commit to anything to try you?

No. Give us one trial container off the next vessel and judge us on the clock. Every account we run today started with a single load.
