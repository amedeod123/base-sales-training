Posting Loads on the DAT Load Board

How to cover a load with a carrier — and price it right.

← Back to training home

1What DAT is & when you'll use it

DAT is the biggest freight load board. Brokers post loads; carriers search for them.

You'll use DAT in three situations:

SituationWhat you do on DAT
You booked freight and need a truckPost the load so carriers find it and call/email you with rates
You're quoting a customer and need to price itCheck DAT Rate View (market rates) so you don't underbid or overpay
You have an empty truck to fill (backhaul)Post truck availability or search loads for that lane
The point of posting a load: get the load in front of hundreds of carriers at once instead of cold-calling them one by one. A well-posted load gets covered fast and at a fair rate.

2How to post a load — step by step

Origin & destination. City/state for pickup and delivery. Be exact — the city drives who sees it.
Equipment type. Pick the right one or the wrong carriers see it: Power Only (carrier brings the tractor only — our port containers), Reefer (refrigerated — bananas/produce), Van (dry boxed freight), Flatbed (lumber, steel, building materials).
Full or Partial. Almost always Full (FTL) for us.
Weight & length. e.g. 40,000 lbs. Don't overstate weight (scares carriers off).
Pickup & delivery dates. The available date(s). Carriers filter by date hard — get it right.
Rate. Post your target rate, or leave blank to collect bids. Anchor high if you post a number — you negotiate down, never up.
Contact info. Your name, phone (carriers call/text first), and email. Make it easy to reach you.
Reference #. Your internal load number so you can match the call to the load.
Comment box. The most important free-text field — see §3.

3The comment box — where you win or lose carriers

It's character-limited (~140). The structured fields cover origin/dest/equipment/rate, so use the comment for what filters and sells the load.

Lead with the deal-breakers (so unqualified carriers self-select out) and the perks (so the right ones lean in):

DEDICATED 40/wk. Power-only, port supplies chassis. TWIC REQUIRED. Drop & hook by 5AM, return empty to Wilmington port. Quick pay.
Put in the comment…Why
TWIC REQUIRED / credentials#1 filter — saves calls from carriers who can't run it
Power-only / chassis suppliedTells them they don't bring a trailer + removes the chassis worry
Drop & hook or live unloadSets the expectation up front
DEDICATED / weekly volumeThe hook — carriers chase recurring lanes, not one-offs
Quick pay + your numberDrives fast response (text beats call for owner-ops)
Don't: dump origin/dest/rate into the comment (they're already structured fields) — you'll waste your 140 characters. And don't advertise a higher weight than the load actually is.

4Price it with DAT Rate View first

Never post a rate blind. Check the market before you quote a customer or pay a carrier.

DAT Rate View shows the recent average spot and contract rate for a lane. Use it to:

  • Quote your customer with confidence (don't underbid your own margin away).
  • Know what carriers expect so you don't overpay — or post a rate so low nobody calls.
  • Spot a good lane — if the market rate is well above what you can cover it for, that's margin.
Rule: you make money on the spread (customer rate − carrier rate). Rate View protects both sides of that spread. Always confirm BASE's floor/target with Amedeo before committing.

5Posting truck availability (backhaul)

The flip side: when we have an empty truck, post the truck to find a load for it.

For the empty southbound leg, post available capacity: origin (e.g. Boston area), destination region (I-95 south), equipment (40' container / van), available date. Shippers and their brokers search trucks the same way carriers search loads.

Broker rule — don't forget it: BASE can only take freight from direct shippers, never from another broker. When a "load" you find on the board is posted by a broker, that's double-brokering — a hard no. Use the board to find shippers, not to resell another broker's freight.

6Handling responses & vetting the carrier

✅ Do

  • Answer fast — first broker to respond often wins the truck
  • Repost to stay near the top of search results
  • Confirm MC/DOT, authority, insurance & TWIC before you commit (ops/the system checks this)
  • Get the rate con signed before the truck rolls

🚫 Don't

  • Post a load you can't actually cover
  • Book a carrier you haven't vetted
  • Quote below BASE's floor without Amedeo
  • Accept a load from another broker
